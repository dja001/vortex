# LaTeX preferences

- Don't add manual spacing adjustments (`\,`, `\!`, `\;`, `\:`, `\quad`, ...)
  to equations and expressions. Leave spacing to LaTeX.
- Line layout of the source:
  - Every sentence starts on a new line.
  - Lines are about 100 characters long. Break a longer sentence with a
    newline at a natural point (after a comma or clause, before inline math).
  - Apply this to the lines you write or modify. Leave untouched lines as they
    are: reformatting them creates merge conflicts with the user's edits.
